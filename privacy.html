Puzzle Jigglets Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Puzzle Jigglets is a jigsaw puzzle app for young children, made by DMR Global ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We wrote it in plain language so parents can read it quickly.

In short: Puzzle Jigglets has no ads, no sign-up, no chat and no tracking. We do not ask children for any personal information. Puzzle progress stays on the device. The only data that leaves the device is what is needed to check whether a grown-up has bought a Jigglets Plus subscription and to download app updates.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings or location.
  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no way for children to talk to other people.
  • The app shows no ads and uses no advertising or analytics tools. It does not use the device's advertising ID.
  • The app does not use the camera or microphone.

Information stored only on the device

The app saves puzzle progress (which puzzles are solved, stars earned, the daily streak and pieces already placed) on the device itself. This information is never sent to us. You can erase it at any time with Reset progress on the home screen, or by uninstalling the app.

Daily reminders

A grown-up can turn on an optional daily puzzle reminder. It is off until a grown-up turns it on. Reminders are scheduled by the phone itself: no information is sent to us or anyone else to make them work. You can turn them off in the app, or in the phone's notification settings.

App updates

To deliver new puzzles, the app checks for updates when it starts, using Expo (EAS Update). This request contains technical information only, such as the app version, the operating system and the IP address of the connection. It contains no information about the child or about how the app is used. Expo processes it under its own privacy policy.

Subscriptions and purchases

Some puzzles are unlocked with an optional Jigglets Plus subscription. Purchases are behind a grown-up check and are handled entirely by Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iPhone and iPad). We never see or store payment card details.

To check whether a subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. When the app starts and when a purchase is made, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random identifier created by the app, which is not linked to the child's or parent's name or email;
  • purchase and subscription status, such as which plan was bought and when it renews or ends;
  • basic technical information needed to deliver the service, such as the app version, the device's operating system, the country of the store account and the IP address of the connection.

This information is used only to unlock the puzzles that were paid for, to restore purchases, and to keep subscription records. It is not used for advertising and is not sold. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy. Google's handling of Google Play purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Puzzle Jigglets is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The random identifier described above does not identify a child and is used only for the subscription check. Links that leave the app and purchase screens can only be opened after a grown-up answers a question.

How long we keep information

Subscription records are kept by RevenueCat while the subscription is active and for as long as needed for purchase records, restoring purchases, and legal or accounting duties. Puzzle progress on the device is kept until you reset it or uninstall the app.

Your choices and deleting data

Parents can ask us to delete the subscription data linked to their app installation by emailing us at the address below. Please include the approximate purchase date, so we can find the record. Cancelling or managing a subscription is done in the Google Play or App Store account settings.

Security

Information sent to RevenueCat is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app uses information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
